Compatibility notes

  • The proven baseline is retained verbatim, including super resolutions and console modes.
  • New game entries are deduplicated by width, height, requested refresh, and scan type.
  • Reported refresh rates are preserved; no forced 60 Hz conversion.
  • Unreported refresh rates and ambiguous scan types are listed for review rather than guessed.
  • Non-8-aligned widths such as 426×240 are listed for CRU/manual review by default.
  • VMMaker may still adjust refresh rates or reject requests to fit your monitor preset. Review generated timings before installation.
